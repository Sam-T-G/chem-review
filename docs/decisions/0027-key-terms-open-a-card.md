# D-0027: Key terms are highlighted, and a student can open each one to learn what it means, with examples

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-16
- Decided by: Sam
- Teams: learning, design, lewis, chem

## Context
Sam wrote, after the glow, the strip of checks and the one-move controls (D-0024 to D-0026) went live:

> lets add some features, i want some key terms and concepts that students may not be familirar with to have highlights so students can click on the terms to be able to learn about and clearly understand what they mean. for example, if students do not know the definition or WHAT a valence electron is, or lone pair, or delta en, etc i want there to be clear examples and clarifications for all of that.

## Decision
- Key terms and concepts that a student may not be familiar with are highlighted.
- A student can click a highlighted term to learn about it and understand clearly what it means.
- Each term has clear examples and clarifications. The terms he names are valence electron, lone pair and ΔEN, and his "etc" leaves the list open.

## Consequences
- Which words count as key terms, what each explanation says, what its examples are and how it looks are the build session's design and wait for Sam's yes (D-0028).
- "etc" is read as: every chemistry term the seven steps use that a first-semester student may not know. A different reading is a question for Sam.
