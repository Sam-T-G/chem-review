# D-NNNN: The decision as one plain sentence

- Status: proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decided by: Sam, or the role that decided and on whose word
- Teams: the team ids this binds, separated by commas

## Context
What made a choice necessary. When the decision is Sam's, quote him word for word:

> his words

Name the options that were looked at and why each was turned down.

## Decision
What was chosen, specific enough that someone could tell whether a change follows it.

## Consequences
What this makes easier, what it makes harder, what it leaves open, and which files or checks carry it.
